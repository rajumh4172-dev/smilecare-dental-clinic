General Dentistry • Preventative Care How often should I visit the dentist for a routine check-up & cleaning?

We recommend visiting SmileCare every 6 months for a comprehensive oral examination and professional ultrasonic cleaning. Routine visits remove hardened tartar (calculus) that normal brushing cannot eliminate and catch micro-cavities before they reach the tooth pulp.

Patients with active gum disease, orthodontic braces, or diabetes benefit from 3-to-4 month visits to safeguard enamel and bone health.

✓ 6-Month Routine Recalls ✓ Ultrasonic Gentle Cleaning ✓ Comprehensive Oral Cancer Screen
Patient Comfort • Anxiety Relief Is dental treatment painful? What if I suffer from dental anxiety?

Dental care should never be painful. At SmileCare, we employ micro-gauge painless needles and topical pre-numbing gels that freeze the gum before any injection. For anxious patients, our team explains each step beforehand with zero rush.

You retain complete control during your appointment: raise your left hand anytime to pause treatment for a break or a sip of water. We also provide ambient headphones, stress-relief squeeze balls, and gentle calming techniques.

✓ Painless Computerized Anesthesia ✓ Hand-Signal Pause Guarantee ✓ Zero-Rushing Gentle Environment
Restorative Care • Implants How do Dental Implants work, and how long do they last?

A dental implant is a biocompatible titanium root surgically secured into the jawbone, fusing with the bone over 8–12 weeks (osseointegration). Once fused, an abutment and custom porcelain crown are affixed to recreate a permanent, life-like tooth.

Unlike traditional bridges, implants do not require shaving down adjacent healthy teeth, and they stimulate natural bone density to preserve facial structure. With regular brushing and dental check-ups, dental implants have a 98%+ clinical success rate and can last a lifetime.

✓ Lifetime Durability ✓ 98%+ Clinical Success Rate ✓ Protects Adjacent Teeth
Emergency Dentistry • Immediate Help What counts as a dental emergency, and do you offer same-day appointments?

Yes! We maintain dedicated emergency triage slots every day. Emergencies include excruciating unremitting toothaches, knocked-out or fractured teeth from trauma, visible facial or gum swelling (abscess), and lost dental crowns exposing sensitive nerves.

Knocked-Out Tooth First-Aid: Handle only by the crown (never the root), gently rinse in clean water, and immerse in cold milk or saliva. Bring it immediately to our clinic within 60 minutes for the highest chance of saving the tooth. Call our hotline at +91 99014 63886.

⚡ Same-Day Emergency Priority ✓ 60-Min Tooth Rescue Window ✓ Direct Doctor Phone Line
Transparent Costs • Financial Flexibility How much does dental treatment cost, and do you accept insurance or EMI plans?

We operate with complete financial transparency. After your diagnosis, you receive a detailed, printed breakdown of treatment fees with zero hidden charges or surprise extras.

We support major health insurance networks for eligible accidental and surgical dental claims. For extensive care (such as multiple implants, clear aligners, or crowns), we offer 0% Interest EMI installment options spread over 3, 6, or 12 monthly payments so finances never compromise your health.

✓ 0% Interest EMI Installments ✓ Cashless Insurance Paperwork ✓ Upfront Written Estimates
Cosmetic Dentistry • Smile Aesthetics What is the difference between In-Clinic Teeth Whitening and home kits?

In-clinic professional whitening uses dental-grade hydrogen peroxide formulation activated by a cool LED accelerator light, brightening teeth by 5 to 8 shades in just 45 minutes. Your gums are carefully shielded with a rubber barrier so no chemicals touch soft tissue.

Drugstore strips and DIY charcoal kits are abrasive to tooth enamel, take weeks to show minor change, and often lead to severe hypersensitivity or uneven blotches. Professional treatment includes a protective potassium fluoride glaze that seals enamel immediately.

✓ 5–8 Shades Whiter ✓ 45-Minute Single Visit ✓ Enamel-Safe Desensitizing Glaze
Kids Care • Pediatric Dentistry At what age should my child have their first visit to the pediatric dentist?

The American Academy of Pediatric Dentistry recommends children visit the dental clinic by their 1st birthday or within 6 months of their first baby tooth erupting. Baby teeth hold space for permanent adult teeth and are prone to nursing bottle decay.

Our first visits are playful "Happy Visits"—featuring gentle chair rides, cartoon stickers, and zero needles. We check tooth eruption, evaluate bite alignment, apply protective fluoride varnish, and coach parents on gentle teething care.

✓ By Age 1 or First Tooth ✓ Kid-Friendly Playful Experience ✓ Cavity Shielding Fluoride Varnishes
Clinical Safety • Modern Technology Are dental X-rays safe? How much radiation do they emit?

Dental X-rays at SmileCare are exceptionally safe. We use cutting-edge digital intraoral sensors and OPG scanners that emit up to 90% less radiation than traditional dental film radiography.

A full digital dental X-ray exposes you to roughly 0.005 millisieverts—equivalent to less than half a day of natural atmospheric background radiation or a short flight. We also supply lead aprons with thyroid collars to ensure zero unnecessary exposure.

✓ 90% Reduced Digital Radiation ✓ Lead Thyroid Protection ✓ Instant High-Def Chairside Imaging
Gum Health • Periodontal Care How do I stop bleeding gums and persistent bad breath (halitosis)?

Bleeding when brushing or flossing is a telltale sign of gingivitis (inflammation caused by bacterial plaque buildup). When tartar hardens along the gumline, bacteria emit volatile sulfur compounds that cause persistent bad breath.

Bleeding is reversed with a gentle professional scaling session to dislodge bacterial deposits, followed by twice-daily 2-minute brushing, daily flossing or water picking, and antibacterial mouth rinses. Leaving gingivitis untreated risks permanent gum recession and bone loss.

✓ Reverses Gingivitis Early ✓ Deep Ultrasonic Scaling ✓ Eliminates Odor-Causing Bacteria
Appointments • Clinic Access Do you accept walk-in patients or do I need to book an appointment in advance?

We welcome walk-ins during open clinic hours (Monday to Saturday, 9:00 AM – 8:00 PM; Sunday, 10:00 AM – 2:00 PM), but we strongly recommend booking in advance.

Pre-scheduled appointments are given first priority with guaranteed zero waiting time in our lobby. You can reserve your preferred time slot and doctor online in under 30 seconds, or send us a quick WhatsApp text at +91 99014 63886.

✓ Guaranteed Zero Wait Time ✓ 30-Second Online Booking ✓ Walk-Ins Welcomed
